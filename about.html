About AI SLOP

← back to main page

AI SLOP is an open place to write about using AI. Not hype and not doom: what it is actually like to build with it, learn with it, argue with it and get annoyed by it.

Why the name?

The internet is filling up with slop. This site is the opposite of that: real people, writing by hand in their own words, about how they really use AI. The good, the bad and the embarrassing.

What you'll find here

  • Honest experiences: what worked, what didn't, and what you'd never try again.
  • Tips and how-tos from people who learned the hard way.
  • Opinions, rants and arguments, as long as they come with a reason.
  • Stories about learning, building and getting stuck, with or without AI.

Open to everyone

Anyone can read. Anyone with an account can write. There are no gatekeepers: sign up, hit write and publish. You can like, dislike, comment and follow the people whose writing you enjoy, and you'll get a notification when someone does the same for you.

Come and contribute

Whether you're a developer, a student, a writer, or just someone who has an opinion about chatbots, your experience is worth sharing. Your first post doesn't have to be long or polished. It just has to be yours.

A few ground rules

  • Write it yourself. If AI helped, say so. That's part of the point.
  • Be honest, and be kind in the comments.
  • Posts that are spam or abuse get removed.
